CMPM 120 — Platformer
Level select
On the title screen, press 1, 2, or 3 to choose which level to play.

Unique mechanic per level
Level 1 — Neil

Lock and key. The goal flag is sealed behind a locked door in the temple wall. The key is on top of the crates in the factory. Without the key, the door won't open and shows a hint. Once you pick up the key (shown in the top-right corner), touching the door unlocks it.

Level 2 Logan

Kill enemies for points. Higher risk, higher reward. You could rush to the goal, or you could explore and get lots of points. 

Level 3 - Andy

Bounce pad. The level features bounce pads which force a jump at 1.25x power.
They are used to gain additional elevation, or as an obstacle which you should avoid to stop yourself from bouncing into a hazard. The bounce pads are mandatory in reading the goal at the top.

Bonus features (all levels)

Complex camera: deadzone with smoothed follow.

Enemies: patrolling walking and flying enemies that turn around at walls and can be stomped from above.

Ways to die: spikes, pits, and touching enemies.

juice: screen shake on death, screen flash on unlock, and confetti on the end screen.
